Got questions?

Frequently asked questions.

Do I need previous experience to apply?

Requirements vary by domain. Review the details for your chosen internship on the official program page before applying.

Are the internships remote?

InternNova advertises virtual internship tracks. Confirm the format and schedule for your selected cohort before enrolling.

Will I receive a certificate?

Certificates depend on completing mandatory tasks and evaluations. See InternNova's official terms for full eligibility rules.

Are internships paid or free?

InternNova lists both free internships and paid premium training programs. Standard internships are generally unpaid unless explicitly stated otherwise; review any fees before enrollment.

Does completing a program guarantee a job?

No. InternNova's terms do not guarantee employment, placement, or interview selection.
